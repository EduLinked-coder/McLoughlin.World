---
title: "Dispute as Protocol, Not Punishment"
date: 2025-01-05
summary: Exploring mediation-first dispute practices for digital guilds.
tags: [protocol, mediation]
---

Conflict within collaborative systems is inevitable, but the response can be designed with care. The EduLinked approach frames dispute resolution as a protocol that reinforces community values rather than a punitive mechanism. This means centering mediation, reflective pause, and restorative action before any sanctions are considered.

By documenting the process transparently and inviting mutual accountability, we transform conflict into an opportunity to reaffirm shared purpose.
