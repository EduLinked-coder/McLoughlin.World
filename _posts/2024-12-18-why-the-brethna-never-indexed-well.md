---
title: "Why the Brethna Never Indexed Well"
date: 2024-12-18
summary: Lessons from archival misalignments in Celtic legal codices.
tags: [knowledge, archives]
---

Historic law tracts like the Brethna are difficult to index because they were never written for linear retrieval. Their structure privileges oral commentary and situational judgement, making modern metadata a poor fit.

Our archival practice respects that original logic by cataloguing stories, relationships, and obligations rather than forcing a rigid taxonomy. Doing so honours the communities that preserved these texts.
