# Glossary of Gatekeeping Devices

**Canonical public URL:** https://www.mcloughlin.world/glossaries/ssa-lexicon/scheme/gatekeeping-devices/

**SSA Ontology release:** `0.1.0`  
**Concept count:** 6

This concept scheme describes deliberate tools, requirements and processes that control who is recognised as legitimate, resourced, qualified or permitted to participate. It treats gatekeeping as infrastructure rather than merely attitude.

The scheme is part of the Strategic Self-Advocacy Ontology and should preserve stable concept identifiers, provenance, attribution and visible release history.

Source: McLoughlin.world site export, 7 August 2026.
