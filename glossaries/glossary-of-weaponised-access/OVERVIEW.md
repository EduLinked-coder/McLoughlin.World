# Glossary of Weaponised Access

**Canonical public URL:** https://www.mcloughlin.world/glossaries/ssa-lexicon/scheme/weaponised-access/

**SSA Ontology release:** `0.1.0`  
**Concept count:** 6

This concept scheme names patterns where the language of access and accommodation is used while reinforcing surveillance, dependency, compliance or gatekeeping rather than genuine autonomy and inclusion.

The scheme is part of the Strategic Self-Advocacy Ontology and should preserve stable concept identifiers, provenance, attribution and visible release history.
