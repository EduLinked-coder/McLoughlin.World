# Strategic Self-Advocacy™ Lexicon

**Canonical public URL:** https://www.mcloughlin.world/glossaries/ssa-lexicon/scheme/strategic-self-advocacy-lexicon/

**SSA Ontology release:** `0.1.0`  
**Concept count:** 5

This concept scheme is the protocol layer of SSA™. Its terms are intended as semantic anchors that protect authorship, lived experience and meaning from dilution, co-option and unauthorised reframing.

The scheme is part of the Strategic Self-Advocacy Ontology and should preserve stable concept identifiers, provenance, attribution and visible release history.
