# Glossary of Narrative Laundering

**Canonical public URL:** https://www.mcloughlin.world/glossaries/ssa-lexicon/scheme/narrative-laundering/

**SSA Ontology release:** `0.1.0`  
**Concept count:** 5

This concept scheme names patterns where lived experience is rewritten to become more palatable, less critical or more commercially useful, including sanitisation, reframing and appropriation under the language of awareness or inclusion.

The scheme is part of the Strategic Self-Advocacy Ontology and should preserve stable concept identifiers, provenance, attribution and visible release history.
