# Glossary of Coercive Collaboration — Overview

**Canonical public URL:**

https://www.mcloughlin.world/glossaries/ssa-lexicon/glossary-of-coercive-collaboration/

## Purpose

The Glossary of Coercive Collaboration describes patterns where participation or collaboration is requested without meaningful consent, authorship or power.

It provides language for distinguishing genuine collaboration from arrangements that appear participatory while preserving asymmetrical control, extraction or decision authority.

## Repository expectations

Future semantic representations should preserve distinctions between:

- invitation and compulsion;
- participation and meaningful consent;
- contribution and authorship;
- consultation and decision authority;
- collaboration and extraction.

Machine-readable representations should not infer coercion from participation alone; context, power, agency and consequences for refusal matter.
