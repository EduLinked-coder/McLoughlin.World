# Glossary of Systemic Bypass

**Canonical public URL:** https://www.mcloughlin.world/glossaries/ssa-lexicon/scheme/systemic-bypass/

**SSA Ontology release:** `0.1.0`  
**Concept count:** 5

This concept scheme maps how systems design around lived experience rather than with it, including patterns where authorities appear to include a person's voice while redirecting, obscuring or neutralising it through process.

The scheme is part of the Strategic Self-Advocacy Ontology and should preserve stable concept identifiers, provenance, attribution and visible release history.
