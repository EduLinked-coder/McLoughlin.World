# Glossary of Weaponised Positivity

**Canonical public URL:** https://www.mcloughlin.world/glossaries/ssa-lexicon/scheme/weaponised-positivity/

**SSA Ontology release:** `0.1.0`  
**Concept count:** 5

This concept scheme names patterns where control is presented as kindness, optimism or reassurance and where expectations of cheerfulness, gratitude or calm are used to suppress discomfort, truth or dissent.

The scheme is part of the Strategic Self-Advocacy Ontology and should preserve stable concept identifiers, provenance, attribution and visible release history.
