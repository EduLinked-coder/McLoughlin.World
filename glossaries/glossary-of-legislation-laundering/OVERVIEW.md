# Glossary of Legislation Laundering

**Canonical public URL:** https://www.mcloughlin.world/glossaries/ssa-lexicon/scheme/legislation-laundering/

**SSA Ontology release:** `0.1.0`  
**Concept count:** 11

This concept scheme names patterns where legislation, frameworks, strategies, agreements and standards multiply without clear accountability, implementation ownership or accessible pathways, allowing governance activity itself to obscure responsibility or simulate progress.

The scheme is part of the Strategic Self-Advocacy Ontology and should preserve stable concept identifiers, provenance, attribution and visible release history.
