# Glossary of Digital Sovereignty — Overview

**Canonical public URL:**

https://www.mcloughlin.world/glossaries/ssa-lexicon/glossary-of-digital-sovereignty/

## Purpose

The Glossary of Digital Sovereignty defines concepts that protect authorship, meaning and lived-experience knowledge in digital systems.

It supports semantic distinctions around authorship, control, provenance, consent, reuse, representation and protection of meaning when knowledge is stored, transformed or processed digitally.

## Repository expectations

Machine-readable representations should preserve:

- authorship and provenance;
- consent and permitted-use context;
- distinctions between access, reuse, adaptation and appropriation;
- semantic integrity of lived-experience-derived knowledge;
- stable identifiers and version history.
