# Glossary of Institutional Mimicry — Overview

**Canonical public URL:**

https://www.mcloughlin.world/glossaries/ssa-lexicon/glossary-of-institutional-mimicry/

## Purpose

The Glossary of Institutional Mimicry describes patterns where systems imitate care, reform, inclusion or accountability without materially changing behaviour, power, access or outcomes.

The glossary supports analysis of apparent institutional change that reproduces the underlying structure it claims to remedy.

## Repository expectations

Future semantic representations should preserve distinctions between:

- stated reform and material change;
- symbolic inclusion and actual access;
- procedural compliance and substantive accountability;
- representation and redistribution of power;
- institutional signalling and observable outcomes.

Machine-readable use should rely on evidence of institutional behaviour and outcomes rather than treating language of care, reform or inclusion as proof either for or against institutional mimicry.
