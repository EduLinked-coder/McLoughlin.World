# Glossary of Digital Extraction

**Canonical public URL:** https://www.mcloughlin.world/glossaries/ssa-lexicon/scheme/digital-extraction/

**SSA Ontology release:** `0.1.0`  
**Concept count:** 5

This concept scheme maps mechanisms through which digital systems harvest lived-experience knowledge as data, dilute it into scalable products or solutions, and separate originators from authorship, funding, visibility or influence.

The scheme is part of the Strategic Self-Advocacy Ontology and should preserve stable concept identifiers, provenance, attribution and visible release history.
