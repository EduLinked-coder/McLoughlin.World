# Glossary of System-Led Distortion

**Canonical public URL:** https://www.mcloughlin.world/glossaries/ssa-lexicon/scheme/system-led-distortion/

**SSA Ontology release:** `0.1.0`  
**Concept count:** 5

This concept scheme names subtle and structural ways systems distort, co-opt or weaponise survival behaviour, especially where institutional rules do not match lived reality and people are penalised for failing to conform to misdesigned models.

The scheme is part of the Strategic Self-Advocacy Ontology and should preserve stable concept identifiers, provenance, attribution and visible release history.
