# Glossary of Structural Disappearance — Overview

**Canonical public URL:**

https://www.mcloughlin.world/glossaries/ssa-lexicon/glossary-of-structural-disappearance/

## Purpose

The Glossary of Structural Disappearance describes administrative and technical designs that make people disappear from access, records, evidence or institutional visibility.

It provides language for analysing how exclusion can occur through information architecture, administrative categorisation, record design, eligibility processes, evidence systems and other structural mechanisms rather than only through explicit refusal.

## Repository expectations

Future semantic representations should preserve distinctions between:

- absence and administratively produced invisibility;
- missing data and excluded data;
- access failure and record failure;
- evidence not supplied and evidence made impossible to supply;
- individual error and repeatable structural design.

Machine-readable systems should not infer structural disappearance from a missing record alone; pattern, process, provenance and system context are necessary.
