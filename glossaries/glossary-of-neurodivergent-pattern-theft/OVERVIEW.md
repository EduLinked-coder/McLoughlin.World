# Glossary of Neurodivergent Pattern Theft

**Canonical public URL:** https://www.mcloughlin.world/glossaries/ssa-lexicon/scheme/neurodivergent-pattern-theft/

**SSA Ontology release:** `0.1.0`  
**Concept count:** 5

This concept scheme names patterns through which neurodivergent survival tools, regulation practices and ways of understanding are renamed, medicalised, commodified or otherwise reused without appropriate credit, consent or compensation.

The scheme is part of the Strategic Self-Advocacy Ontology and should preserve stable concept identifiers, provenance, attribution and visible release history.

Source: McLoughlin.world site export, 7 August 2026.
