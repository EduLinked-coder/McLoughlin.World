# Glossary of Extractive Allyship

**Canonical public URL:** https://www.mcloughlin.world/glossaries/ssa-lexicon/scheme/extractive-allyship/

**SSA Ontology release:** `0.1.0`  
**Concept count:** 5

This concept scheme names patterns where claimed support, amplification or allyship benefits the supporter while erasing, controlling or extracting from the people whose lived experience is being used.

The scheme is part of the Strategic Self-Advocacy Ontology and should preserve stable concept identifiers, provenance, attribution and visible release history.

Source: McLoughlin.world site export, 7 August 2026.
