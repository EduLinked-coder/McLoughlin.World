**Canonical public URL:**

## Purpose

The Glossary of Accessibility Abuse describes how accessibility language, processes and systems can be used in ways that create control, extraction or dependency rather than meaningful access.

The glossary belongs to the SSA Lexicon and should be treated as part of McLoughlin.world's controlled-vocabulary and semantic-infrastructure layer.
