# Glossary of Co-opted Patterns

**Canonical public URL:** https://www.mcloughlin.world/glossaries/ssa-lexicon/scheme/co-opted-patterns/

**SSA Ontology release:** `0.1.0`  
**Concept count:** 6

This concept scheme names patterns through which lived-experience language, analysis or survival knowledge is reworded, diluted, repackaged or absorbed by institutions. The live glossary describes the vocabulary as a semantic firewall against pattern theft, narrative laundering and reworded harm.

The scheme is part of the Strategic Self-Advocacy Ontology and should preserve stable concept identifiers, provenance, attribution and visible release history.
