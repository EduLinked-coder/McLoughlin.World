Strategic Self-Advocacy Ontology

The canonical entry point for the Strategic Self-Advocacy ontology and its ontology-backed lexicon of institutional, narrative, access, control, erasure, appropriation and systemic patterns.

Ontology status

The SSA ontology is currently represented as a draft semantic architecture. Its public glossary pages remain the authoritative human-readable sources for published terms and definitions.

Authorship and provenance

Created by Sarah Ailish McLoughlin. The formal citation record begins in 2017. Term-level provenance remains linked to each originating SSA Lexicon glossary page.
